## Credit Risk Assessment
RWE is described as investment grade by external rating agencies, including BBB+ with a Stable Outlook (Fitch) and Baa2 with a Stable Outlook (Moody’s). FY2024 profitability metrics retrieved include net income of €5,135 million and adjusted EBITDA of €5,680 million, alongside negative free cash flow of €4,106 million and capital expenditure of €11,240 million. Balance-sheet strength indicators retrieved include total equity of €33,623 million as of 31 Dec 2024 and cash and cash equivalents of €5,090 million at the same date; tangible net worth was computed at €23,373 million for FY2024. Qualitatively, sources highlight material exposure to wholesale electricity price uncertainty and high regulatory/political risk classification (as of Feb 2025), with mitigants including hedging, collateral/credit limits, and access to syndicated credit facilities.
