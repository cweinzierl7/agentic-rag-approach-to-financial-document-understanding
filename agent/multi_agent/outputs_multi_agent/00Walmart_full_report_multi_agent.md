Walmart Inc. reports very large scale revenue (USD 680,985 million in FY2025) and positive profitability (net income USD 20,157 million in FY2025), with positive free cash flow across FY2023–FY2025 (USD 12.0–15.1 billion). Liquidity disclosures include cash and cash equivalents of USD 9,037 million as of January 31, 2025 and undrawn committed lines of credit of USD 15.0 billion as of the same date. External ratings disclosed as of January 31, 2025 indicate high-grade credit quality (e.g., S&P long-term debt AA; Moody’s Aa2; Fitch AA). Key risk exposures described in filings include intense competition, macro sensitivity (disposable income/credit availability/GDP), cybersecurity and third-party risks, and broad regulatory/legal compliance.
